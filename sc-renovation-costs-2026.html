Why Crawl Space Matters in SC

  • South Carolina's average relative humidity exceeds 70% in summer months
  • Crawl space moisture can increase energy bills by 15–25%
  • Encapsulation can reduce floor-level allergens and improve indoor air quality
  • Homes in Columbia, Lexington, and Chapin are especially susceptible due to clay soil and water table levels

Year-Over-Year Trends (2023–2026)

Home renovation costs have shifted significantly over the past four years:

  • 2023: Post-pandemic material costs stabilizing — lumber down 40% from 2022 highs
  • 2024: Labor shortages easing — contractor availability improved 15–20%
  • 2025: Material costs stabilizing — composite materials and LVP gaining market share over traditional hardwood
  • 2026: Minor remodels ROI at all-time highs (70–96%), mid-range projects dominating
